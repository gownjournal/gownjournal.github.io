## The architecture finding

The studios ranking for these terms do not run a site. They run **one page per
search intent**. Clair Bridal is the clearest case, holding at least eight:

```
/שמלות-כלה-בוהו-שיק/      /שמלת-כלה-פשוטה/
/שמלות-כלה-נסיכותיות/     /שמלות-כלה-למלאות/
/שמלות-כלה-זולות/         /מחיר-שמלת-כלה/
/שמלות-כלה-בכפר-סבא/      /שמלות-כלה/  (catalogue)
```

Anabel Bolotov runs two separate boho pages. Oshi and Karin Bar each hold
several style terms. Shir Gindi runs a **separate domain** for a campaign
landing page — `shirgindii.com`, titled "LP".

That last one answers a question this business has open: a leading studio in
the same category chose a standalone address for its campaign page over
embedding it in the main site.

## Price positioning

Publishing the price in the title is the norm here, not the exception.

- Clair: `5,900 ₪ בלבד!` in every title, and a dedicated `/מחיר-שמלת-כלה/` page
- Mika: `3,990 ₪`, short dresses `2,700 ₪`
- Oshi: "במחירים משתלמים"
- Market range seen in results: **2,500–6,000 ₪**

Shany Sasson's own Wix page is titled *"שמלות כלה בוהו — תפירה אישית במחיר
שפוי"* — competing on the price axis in words while withholding the number.
The owner has decided against publishing a range; that decision stands. The
observation is recorded because "במחיר שפוי" without a figure is the weakest
position on that axis: it raises the question and declines to answer it.

## The unclaimed ground

**International shipping.** None of the nine mentions shipping abroad. Shany
Sasson ships to the United States and Europe and runs an English Shopify store
in USD, and no Hebrew page says so. This is the only differentiator in the set
that is both real and uncontested.

**The city term.** Clair holds כפר סבא, Karin Bar holds ראש העין, Anabel holds
נשר. Nothing was found holding **הוד השרון**.

## What competitors say happens at a fitting

Worth knowing because it is the section every one of these pages has:

- Shir Gindi: a personal introductory meeting, then fittings through to the
  wedding day
- Anabel: trying each dress on, the feel of French lace, handwork
- TOORA: phone booking, handmade

The three-stage structure on this studio's own pages matches what the category
does. That was not a lucky guess, and it is not a reason to change it.
