# Privacy

The Gown Journal is a static website. It has no accounts, no comment system, no newsletter database and no advertising.

## What is collected

- **Server logs.** The hosting provider records standard access logs (IP address, page requested, browser type, time). These are used only to keep the site running and are not shared with us in identifiable form.
- **Fonts.** Typefaces are loaded from Google Fonts, which means your browser requests font files from Google's servers. Google's privacy policy applies to that request.
- **Analytics.** None at the time of writing. If privacy-respecting analytics are added later, this page will be updated first.

## Cookies

The site sets no cookies of its own.

## Email

If you write to us, we keep your message for as long as needed to reply and to act on any correction. We do not add you to any list.

## Changes

This page carries the date of its last update in the page source. Material changes will be noted here.
