# Contact

We read everything. Three kinds of message are especially welcome:

- **Corrections.** If a guide gets something wrong, tell us which guide and what the error is. Verified corrections are made promptly and noted on the page.
- **Questions we have not answered.** If you searched for something and found nothing useful here, that is a guide we should probably write.
- **Professional input.** If you make wedding dresses for a living and disagree with something we have published, we would like to hear why.

Email: <a href="mailto:{{ site.email }}">{{ site.email }}</a>

We do not answer requests to recommend a specific salon, designer or brand, and we do not accept sponsored content. See our [editorial policy](/editorial-policy/).
