# Editorial policy

## Independence

The Gown Journal is not owned by, funded by, or affiliated with any bridal salon, designer, manufacturer or marketplace. We accept no sponsored posts, no paid placements and no affiliate commissions. If a guide ever includes a commercial relationship of any kind, it will be disclosed at the top of that guide in plain language.

## How guides are researched

Each guide starts with the questions brides actually ask, gathered from conversations, forums and salon staff. The draft is then checked with at least one professional who works with wedding dresses daily: a designer, a pattern cutter or a seamstress. Where professionals disagree (they often do, on things like how tight a bodice should be or whether a veil should match the lace), the guide reports the disagreement rather than resolving it artificially.

## What we do not do

- We do not print prices or price ranges.
- We do not rank, review or recommend specific businesses.
- We do not use stock photography presented as real dresses.
- We do not publish content generated without editorial review.

## Corrections and updates

Guides carry a publication date and, when revised, an update date. If you find an error, tell us through the [contact page](/contact/). Corrections of fact are made as soon as they are verified, and material changes are noted in the guide.

## Language

Guides are written in plain English. We avoid salon jargon where a plain word exists, and we explain it where it does not. Nothing on this site is a substitute for a fitting with a professional who can see you in the dress.
